43Tracker

Privacy Policy

A Chrome extension that tracks your deck, graveyard, banished pile, and opponent reveals while you duel on Duelingbook.

Effective September 2, 2026

The short version: 43Tracker reads the duel you're already playing and keeps a running count on your screen. Nothing it reads ever leaves your device. There is no account, no analytics, and no server operated by 43Tracker.

01

What 43Tracker does

43Tracker is a side-panel extension for duelingbook.com. Its single purpose is live duel tracking: while you play, it reads the game state already rendered in your browser — your remaining deck, graveyard, and banished cards, sorted by Monster / Spell / Trap — and displays a running count. It also lets you import a decklist for comparison and log opponent cards revealed during the match (for example, by Trap Dustshoot).

02

What it reads, and why

To do this, 43Tracker's content scripts read card names and counts directly out of the Duelingbook page you're viewing — the same information already visible to you on screen. Chrome classifies this as website content, which is why this policy exists. It does not read your Duelingbook password, your messages, or any part of the page unrelated to the duel in progress.

03

Where it's stored

Everything 43Tracker reads is written to chrome.storage.local — Chrome's on-device storage for extensions. It stays on your machine, moving only between the extension's own scripts and its side panel, so the panel can display it. It is cleared automatically when a new duel starts, and entirely when you remove the extension.

04

The one outside request it makes

43Tracker fetches public card metadata — names and images, not personal data — from the YGOPRODeck API (db.ygoprodeck.com) to display cards in an imported decklist. No duel data, hand contents, or identifying information is sent as part of that request.

06

Sharing and sale

  • 43Tracker does not sell or transfer user data to third parties.
  • Data is never used for any purpose outside the single tracking function described above.
  • Data is never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.
07

Your controls

Since everything lives in local browser storage, clearing it is entirely in your hands: it resets automatically at the start of each new duel, or you can remove all of it at once by uninstalling the extension from chrome://extensions.

08
